## Defects fixed on the way

- `EfUserRepository.UpdateAsync` replaced the concurrency stamp before attaching a detached user, so every
  detached update failed its own concurrency check.
- `LocalFileBlobRepository.ListAsync("folder/")` threw on the trailing slash its own quickstart used.
- `Argon2PasswordHasher` verified with hard-coded cost, so raising the cost would have locked out every user.
- Dapper `UpdateAsync` / `DeleteAsync` ignored every concurrency token, so a stale writer silently won.
- Dapper could not insert an `IVersioned` row on PostgreSQL: Npgsql rejects `uint` parameters.
- HybridCache rethrew `JsonException` for an old-shape L2 entry on every read until it expired.
